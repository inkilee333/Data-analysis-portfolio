# Data-analysis-portfolio
Portfolio of various methods of data analysis and visualization from PhD work and personal interests!

- NVIDIA stock analysis (time series, volatility, correlation)

In this project, I conduct a time series analysis on NVIDIA stocks over the past 5 years to model the mean equation and heteroscedastic volatility of daily returns. I find that the price of NVDA stocks over time follows a random walk with drift, and that the volatility contains correlations over weekly and monthly periods of time. I model the volatility using a GARCH(1,1) model and use it to forecast the next 5 days of stocks. Based on the data, I make inferences on NVIDIA's correlations to the tech/AI industry before and after NVIDIA's momentum-driven rise.
